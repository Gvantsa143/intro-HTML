Introduction to Tarot

There's many tools that are used for divination and fortune-telling, such as crystals, pendulums, runes, tarot cards and etc.
Today we're going to be talking about one of the most used divinations "Tarot Cards".
Tarot cards are divided in two main sections: The Major Arcana and The Minor Arcana

The Major Arcana

The Major Arcana represent universal archetypes, major life transitions and spiritual lessons known as "The Fool's Journey".

  • The Magician (I)
  • The High Priestess (II)
  • The Empress (III)

  • The Lovers (VI)
  • The Chariot (VII)
  • Strength (VIII)
  • The Hermit (IX)
  • Wheel of Fortune (X)
  • Justice (XI)
  • The Hanged Man (XII)
  • Death (XIII)
  • Temperance (XIV)
  • The Devil (XV)
  • The Tower (XVI)
  • The Star (XVII)
  • The Moon (XVIII)
  • The Sun (XIX)
  • Judgement (XX)
  • The World (XXI)

The Minor Arcana

The Minor Arcana represent the daily events, practical circumstances and temporary influences of everyday life.
The Minor Arcana is split into four suits with 14 cards in each suit.

    • Swords
